Design system exploration

One restaurant journey, shaped to every screen.

This protected concept explores content-first continuity, restrained translucent controls, and layouts that expand without duplicating state or actions. It borrows principles, not proprietary assets or exact interface patterns.

Glass is a control layer, not a decoration layer. Listings, maps, and evidence stay solid and readable. Frosted treatment is reserved for commands, navigation, and the compact selected-place sheet.
